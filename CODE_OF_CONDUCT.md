# Code of conduct

Treviko welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities, experience levels and approaches to fitness. Treat contributors and users with patience and respect.

## Expected behavior

Give constructive, specific feedback. Discuss ideas without attacking people. Respect names, pronouns, accessibility needs and personal boundaries. Make room for beginners and accept corrections graciously.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, sexually demean, shame bodies or abilities, publish someone else's private information, or repeatedly disrupt discussions. Do not pressure anyone to disclose health, dietary or training information.

These expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related community spaces, including when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the repository owner privately through an established trusted channel. Do not post private evidence or personal contact details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

A dedicated confidential conduct contact must be confirmed by the owner before public launch; none is invented here. Until then, do not publish sensitive reports to seek that contact.

Maintainers may ask for a change in behavior, edit or remove inappropriate material, close a discussion, or restrict participation based on the situation. Reports should be handled with discretion; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where action requires disclosure or applicable obligations require it. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is unacceptable.
